**Lipids** are a large *grouping* of compounds which are all hydrophobic, and are not true [[Polymers and Macromolecules|polymers]]. They are varied in form and function, some of which including waxes and pigments, but the most important biological classes are [[#Fats|fats]], [[#Phospholipids|phospholipids]], and [[#Steroids|steroids]].


## Fats

**Fats** consist of three long, non-polar hydrocarbon chains (**fatty acids**) attached to a **glycerol**, which has a carbon structure with 3 hydroxyl groups that the fatty acids bond to via a [[Polymers and Macromolecules|dehydration reaction]] forming **ester linkages**. In some of the fatty acid hydrocarbon chains, the carbon atoms can bond to each other resulting in *cis* double bond. This double bond creates a bend in the chain, altering the properties of the fatty molecule.

![[Pasted image 20251114184204.png]]

Fats without these double bonds are called **saturated fats** because the carbon chain is saturated with hydrogen, resulting in a linear structure that can be packed tightly together. These types of fats form *solids* such as lard and butter. Conversely, fats with the *cis* double carbon bonds and subsequent bend cannot form structures as easily, and thus do not pack together, instead forming a *liquid*, which we often refer to as oils. These are called **unsaturated fats** because the carbon chains are not fully saturated with hydrogen atoms due to the double bonds. 

![[Pasted image 20251114184216.png]]

Organisms typically use fats as energy storage since the long hydrocarbon fatty acids contain lots of energy. A gram of fat stores over twice as much energy as a gram of [[Carbohydrates|polysaccharide]]. Fats are more common in mammals than plants due to the higher energy density, and also serve the purpose of cushioning other organs and skin, as well as providing insulation. 


## Phospholipids

**Phospholipids** are similar to fats but only have two fatty acids attached to the glycerol, with a **phosphate group** bonded to the third carbon. Importantly, whereas the two fatty acids are still non-polar, the phosphate group is *negatively charged*, and may also bond to another polar molecule, which will further alter its properties. The phosphate group forms the phospholipids **hydrophilic head**, and the fatty acids form its **hydrophobic tail**. When exposed to water, phospholipids  self organize into double sided sheets called a *bilayer*.

![[Pasted image 20251114185332.png]]

This arrangement makes up the **phospholipid bilayer** of the walls of cells and organelles, which is fundamental to cellular biology.


## Steroids

Steroids are lipids characterized by a carbon skeleton made up of four fused rings. Different chemical groups can bond to the ring, defining the steroids form and function.
