The term **mycorrhiza** refers to the *symbiotic relationship* between a [[Morphology of Mycelium|mycelial]] fungus network and the root system of a plant. In these relationships, one or more mycorrhizal fungi will grow adjacently or intracellularly with the roots of one or more plants.

The fungi perform the extracellular digestion that breaks down the rocky substrate (or decays organic matter), and provides those minerals and water to the plant which then photosynthesizes and returns sugars and lipids.

**Arbuscular mycorrhiza** are types of fungi that penetrate the cell walls of cortical cells in roots of types vascular plants forming arbuscules. **Ectomycorrhiza** form on the outside of the root of many woody plants and **Ericoid mycorrhiza** form on the outside of plants in the Ericaceae family. There are many diverse and specialized mycorrhizal relationships.

Mycorrhizal relationships are fundamental to the evolution of plants, over 90 percent of plant species depend on mycorrhizae.
