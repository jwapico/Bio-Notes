Ribosomes conduct protein synthesis within the cytosol of the cell or bound to a membrane. *Free ribosomes* float freely in the cytosol and generally produce proteins/enzymes that float freely in the cytosol, and *bound ribosomes* are attached to the [[Endoplasmic Reticulum|endoplasmic reticulum]] or [[Nucleus|nuclear envelope]], and typically produce membrane bound proteins. Bound and free ribosomes are structurally identical, and can be found at either location at different times.
