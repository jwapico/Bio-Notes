These are Latin names for testing methods

**Vivo, Ex-Vivo (within the living):** Experiments conducted in or out of a living body, respectively

**In Vitro (in glass, in the glass):** Experiments conducted with an organism inside a medium prepared artificially such as in a petri dish 

**In Situ (in place, on site):** Studies conducted on phenomena occurring in their original context or natural environment.
