In eukaryotic cells, [[Mitochondria|mitochondria]] and [[Chloroplasts|chloroplasts]] are semi-autonomous, double-membrane bound organelles responsible for converting chemical or light energy to a form that can be transported and used within the cell. Both of these organelles contain their own [[DNA]] encoding their own [[The Endomembrane System#Ribosomes|ribosomes]], and organelle proteins to be synthesized via those ribosomes. These organelles grow and reproduce on their own within the cell, leading to the logical **endosymbiont** theory which states that these organelles are descendent from *prokaryotes* that were engulfed at some point and evolved to form a symbiotic relationship with the larger cell.
