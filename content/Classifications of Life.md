According to *Campbell Biology 12th Edition*, all of life can be classified into three domains: Bacteria, Archaea, and Eukarya. 

Bacteria and Archaea are prokaryotic, differing in membrane composition, genetic machinery, and habitat. Eukarya is subclassified into: kingdom Plantae, kingdom Fungi, kingdom Animalia, and protists (which are single celled ?). Bacteria are also subclassified into kingdoms.
