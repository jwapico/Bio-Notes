The **endomembrane system** is a network of membrane bound organelles responsible for protein synthesis and transport, lipid metabolism and transport, and the detoxification of harmful molecules. The membranes of this system are related either through direct physical continuity or by the transport of vesicle bound products via the [[Cytoskeleton|cytoskeleton]], and take on diverse forms, functions, and compositions that may evolve over time.

The endomembrane system consists primarily of the [[Nucleus|nucleus]],  [[Endoplasmic Reticulum|endoplasmic reticulum (ER)]], and [[Golgi Apparatus|Golgi apparatus]], as well as involving [[Ribosomes|ribosomes]], [[Lysosomes|lysosomes]], and [[Vacuoles|vacuoles]].
