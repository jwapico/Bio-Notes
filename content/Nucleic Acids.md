**Nucleic acids** are large [[Polymers and Macromolecules|polymers]] called **polynucleotides** made of individual **nucleotides.**  A nucleotide is made of a 5 carbon sugar skeleton (pentose), a nitrogenous base encoding genetic information, and phosphate groups [[Chemical Bonds#Covalent Bonds|covalently bonding]] with the sugar(s).

![[Pasted image 20251202184314.png]]

There are two classes of nitrogenous bases, *pyrimidines* and *purines.* **Pyrimidines** consist of a carbon skeleton with two nitrogen atoms, while **purines** have *two* carbon rings with nitrogen atoms.
